# Startup Legal & Compliance Requirements — India
Source: Ministry of Corporate Affairs, DPIIT, GST Portal
Last Verified: 2024

---

## Business Registration Options in India

### 1. Private Limited Company (Most Recommended for Startups)

**Governing Law:** Companies Act, 2013
**Authority:** Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) — mca.gov.in

**Advantages:**
- Limited liability for shareholders
- Eligible for DPIIT Startup India recognition
- Easier to raise equity funding
- Separate legal entity
- Can have up to 200 shareholders

**Requirements:**
- Minimum 2 directors (at least 1 Indian resident)
- Minimum 2 shareholders
- Registered office address in India
- Minimum paid-up capital: No minimum (as of 2015 amendment)

**Registration Steps:**
1. Obtain Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) for directors
2. Apply for Director Identification Number (DIN)
3. Name reservation via RUN (Reserve Unique Name)
4. File SPICe+ form on MCA portal
5. Obtain Certificate of Incorporation
6. Apply for PAN and TAN
7. Open bank account

**Time:** 10-15 working days
**Cost (approx):** INR 8,000 – INR 20,000 (professional fees + government fees)

---

### 2. Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)

**Governing Law:** LLP Act, 2008
**Authority:** MCA (mca.gov.in)

**Advantages:**
- Simpler compliance vs Private Limited Company
- Limited liability
- Flexibility in management

**Limitations:**
- Cannot issue equity shares — harder to raise VC funding
- Not eligible for some startup tax benefits

**Best For:** Service businesses, professional firms, small teams

---

### 3. Sole Proprietorship

**Advantages:** Simple, low cost, full control
**Limitations:** Unlimited personal liability, not suitable for scaling
**Best For:** Very early stage, solopreneurs testing ideas

## Tax Compliance

### GST (Goods and Services Tax)
**Authority:** GST Council / State GST departments
**Website:** https://www.gst.gov.in

**Registration Required When:**
- Annual turnover exceeds INR 40 lakh (goods) or INR 20 lakh (services)
- For e-commerce sellers: mandatory regardless of turnover
- Interstate supply: mandatory regardless of turnover

**GST Rates:** 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28% depending on goods/services

### Income Tax
**Authority:** Income Tax Department
**Website:** https://www.incometax.gov.in

**Startup Tax Benefits:**
- Section 80-IAC: 3-year tax holiday for eligible DPIIT-recognised startups (on profits)
- Eligibility: Incorporated between April 1, 2016 and March 31, 2025; annual turnover < INR 100 crore
- Application: Through Startup India portal with relevant financials

### TDS (Tax Deducted at Source)
- Must register for TAN (Tax Deduction Account Number)
- TDS applicable on salaries, contractor payments, rent, professional fees

## Employment Laws

### Key Applicable Laws:
- **The Code on Wages, 2019** — minimum wages, equal remuneration
- **The Code on Social Security, 2020** — PF, ESI, gratuity
- **The Code on Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions, 2020**
- **The Industrial Relations Code, 2020**

### Provident Fund (PF):
- Mandatory for establishments with 20+ employees
- Employee contribution: 12% of basic salary
- Employer contribution: 12% (EPF + EPS)
- Authority: EPFO (https://www.epfindia.gov.in)

### Employee State Insurance (ESI):
- Mandatory for establishments with 10+ employees (in most states)
- Applicable when employee salary ≤ INR 21,000/month
- Authority: ESIC (https://www.esic.gov.in)

## Intellectual Property

### Trademarks
**Authority:** Office of the Controller General of Patents, Designs and Trademarks
**Website:** https://ipindia.gov.in
**Process:** File application on IP India portal
**Timeline:** 18-24 months for registration
**Cost:** INR 4,500 (startups/individuals) to INR 9,000 per class
**Tip:** File early — protection from date of application

### Patents
**Authority:** Indian Patent Office (IPO)
**Website:** https://ipindia.gov.in
**DPIIT Startup Benefit:** 80% fee rebate on patent filing + fast-track examination
**Types:** Provisional application (12 months protection) + Complete application

### Copyright
**Authority:** Copyright Office, Ministry of Education
**Registration:** Not mandatory but recommended
**Protection:** Automatic upon creation; registration aids in disputes

## Data Protection

### Information Technology (IT) Act, 2000
- Relevant for digital/tech startups
- Section 43A: Compensation for failure to protect sensitive personal data
- Section 72A: Punishment for disclosure of information without consent

### Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023
- India's new comprehensive data protection law
- Data principals (users) have rights over their personal data
- Data fiduciaries (companies) must obtain consent and implement safeguards
- Significant Data Fiduciaries face additional compliance requirements
- Penalties: Up to INR 250 crore for breaches

### Practical Steps for Startups:
1. Create a clear Privacy Policy
2. Obtain user consent before collecting personal data
3. Implement data security measures
4. Appoint a Data Protection Officer (if required)
5. Have a clear data retention and deletion policy

## E-Commerce / Digital Startup Specific

### Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020
- Mandatory grievance redressal mechanism
- Display seller information, prices, return policy
- No fake reviews or manipulative practices

### RBI Guidelines (FinTech / Payments)
- Payment aggregators need RBI authorisation
- Lending platforms need NBFC registration or partnership with licensed entity
- Crypto and digital assets have specific RBI advisories

## Startup-Specific Legal Checklist

1. ✅ Register business entity (Pvt Ltd recommended)
2. ✅ Apply for DPIIT Startup Recognition
3. ✅ Obtain PAN and TAN
4. ✅ Register for GST (if applicable)
5. ✅ Open business bank account
6. ✅ Draft Founder Agreement / Shareholders Agreement
7. ✅ Create Employment Agreements / Contractor Agreements
8. ✅ File trademark application for brand name/logo
9. ✅ Create Privacy Policy and Terms of Service
10. ✅ Register on Udyam portal (for MSME benefits)
11. ✅ Maintain proper books of accounts from day one
12. ✅ File annual returns with MCA and Income Tax Department
