# Government Startup Schemes — India
Source: Startup India Official Portal (startupindia.gov.in)
Last Verified: 2024

## Startup India Recognition

**Organisation:** DPIIT (Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade), Ministry of Commerce and Industry
**Website:** https://www.startupindia.gov.in

**Description:**
Startup India is a flagship initiative of the Government of India to build a strong startup ecosystem. DPIIT Recognition is the first step that unlocks all other government startup benefits.

**Eligibility:**
- Entity should be incorporated as a Private Limited Company, Registered Partnership Firm, or LLP
- Age of the entity should not exceed 10 years from date of incorporation
- Annual turnover should not exceed INR 100 crore in any preceding financial year
- Entity should work towards innovation, development, or improvement of products/processes/services
- Should not have been formed by splitting/reconstructing an existing business

**Benefits:**
- Self-certification under 9 labour and 3 environmental laws
- Fast-track patent examination (80% fee rebate on patent filing)
- Tax exemption under Section 80-IAC
- Exemption from Angel Tax (Section 56(2)(viib))
- Easier public procurement (no prior experience/turnover requirement)
- Access to Startup India portal and events
- INR 10 lakh credit guarantee scheme access

**Relevant Startup Stage:** Ideation, Validation, MVP, Growth

**How to Apply:** Visit https://www.startupindia.gov.in/content/sih/en/startupgov/startup-recognition.html and apply with CIN/LLPIN, PAN, and pitch deck.

## Atal Innovation Mission (AIM)

**Organisation:** NITI Aayog
**Website:** https://aim.gov.in

**Description:**
AIM is the Government of India's flagship initiative to promote innovation and entrepreneurship through various programs.

**Key Programs:**
1. **Atal Incubation Centres (AICs)** — Grants up to INR 10 crore to set up world-class incubation centres
2. **Atal Tinkering Labs (ATLs)** — For school-level innovation
3. **Atal New India Challenges** — Problem-based challenges with grants up to INR 1 crore

**Eligibility:** 
- AICs: Educational/research institutions, companies
- New India Challenges: Startups and SMEs with product prototypes

**Benefits:**
- Grant funding (non-dilutive)
- Incubation support
- Mentorship and networking
- Government procurement opportunities

**Relevant Stage:** Ideation, Validation, MVP

## SIDBI — Startup Mitra / Fund of Funds

**Organisation:** Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI)
**Website:** https://www.sidbi.in

**Description:**
SIDBI manages the Fund of Funds for Startups (FFS) under the Startup India initiative — a corpus of INR 10,000 crore to support SEBI-registered Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs) that invest in startups.

**Startup Mitra:** Platform to connect startups with incubators and accelerators.

**Eligibility:**
- DPIIT-recognised startups
- Early to growth stage companies

**Benefits:**
- Equity funding through AIFs
- Soft loans at concessional rates
- Credit guarantee

**Relevant Stage:** Validation, MVP, Growth

## MSME Schemes

**Organisation:** Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises
**Website:** https://msme.gov.in

### Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE)
- Collateral-free loans up to INR 2 crore for micro and small enterprises
- Credit guarantee coverage of 75-85%

### Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP)
- Maximum project cost: INR 50 lakh (Manufacturing), INR 20 lakh (Services)
- Subsidy: 15-35% depending on category and location
- For new micro enterprises only

### Technology Upgradation Fund Scheme (TUFS)
- Interest/capital subsidy for technology upgradation in textile, jute, handicrafts sectors

**Eligibility:** Registered as Micro, Small, or Medium Enterprise on Udyam portal (udyamregistration.gov.in)

## Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY)

**Organisation:** MUDRA Bank / Banks and NBFCs
**Website:** https://www.mudra.org.in

**Description:**
Provides loans up to INR 10 lakh to non-corporate, non-farm small/micro enterprises.

**Categories:**
- **Shishu:** Loans up to INR 50,000
- **Kishore:** Loans from INR 50,001 to INR 5 lakh
- **Tarun:** Loans from INR 5 lakh to INR 10 lakh

**Eligibility:**
- Non-corporate small businesses, traders, artisans, manufacturers
- Good credit history

**Relevant Stage:** Ideation, Validation

## National Science and Technology Entrepreneurship Development Board (NSTEDB)

**Organisation:** Department of Science & Technology (DST), Government of India
**Website:** https://nstedb.com

**Description:**
Promotes tech entrepreneurship and self-employment through Science & Technology Entrepreneurship Parks (STEPs) and Technology Business Incubators (TBIs).

**Programs:**
- Technology Business Incubators (TBI) — grants up to INR 4 crore
- Seed Support System (SSS) — seed funding for startups in TBIs
- NIDHI (National Initiative for Developing and Harnessing Innovations)

**Eligibility:** Deep-tech, science and technology based startups

## Agricultural Technology Schemes

### PM-KISAN (PM Kisan Samman Nidhi)
- Direct income support to farmers
- Relevant for agri-tech startups to understand farmer segments

### RKVY-RAFTAAR (Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana)
- Agri-startup incubation and funding
- Up to INR 25 lakh per agri-startup
- Website: https://rkvy.nic.in

### National Agriculture Market (e-NAM)
- Digital platform for agricultural commodities trading
- API integration opportunities for agri-tech startups

## Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS)

**Organisation:** DPIIT
**Website:** https://seedfund.startupindia.gov.in

**Description:**
Provides financial assistance to startups for proof of concept, prototype development, product trials, market entry, and commercialisation.

**Funding:**
- Up to INR 20 lakh for PoC/prototype/product development
- Up to INR 50 lakh for market entry/commercialisation (as convertible debentures or equity)

**Eligibility:**
- DPIIT-recognised startup incorporated not more than 2 years ago
- Must be incubated at an AIM-supported incubator or DPIIT-empanelled incubator

**Relevant Stage:** Ideation, Validation, MVP

## Tamil Nadu State Government Schemes — StartupTN (TANSIM)

**Organisation:** Tamil Nadu Startup and Innovation Mission (StartupTN / TANSIM), Government of Tamil Nadu
**Website:** https://startuptn.in

### TANSEED (Tamil Nadu Startup Seed Grant Fund)
- **Grant Amount:** Up to INR 10 Lakhs (equity-free seed grant)
- **Eligibility:** Startups registered with StartupTN, working in Tamil Nadu, green technology, agritech, deeptech, social impact, or rural empowerment.
- **Application:** Online via startuptn.in portal. Multiple cohorts announced annually.
- **Benefits:** Non-dilutive grant, incubation access across Tamil Nadu Regional Hubs (Madurai, Tirunelveli, Coimbatore, Erode, Salem, Cuddalore, Thanjavur), mentorship from industry experts.

### Tamil Nadu Agritech / Rural Impact Startup Support
- Supported through TNAU (Tamil Nadu Agricultural University) Agribusiness Incubation Society (TNAU-TBI / EDII-Periyakulam).
- Specialized funding up to INR 25 Lakhs under RKVY-RAFTAAR and TNAU agri-incubation.
- Access to Tamil Nadu agricultural extension centers, farmer producer organizations (FPOs), and regional soil/crop testing networks.

## Education & Career Platform Schemes — EdTech & Skill Development

**Organisation:** Ministry of Education & Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (MSDE)
**Websites:** https://www.msde.gov.in, https://neat.aicte-india.org, https://www.pmkvyofficial.org

### NEAT (National Educational Alliance for Technology) — AICTE
- Scheme to bring best AI-driven EdTech products to college students.
- Government provides EdTech solutions free of cost to economically disadvantaged students, with EdTech startups receiving government partnership and student reach.

### Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY) & Skill India Digital
- National skill certification and job readiness scheme.
- Startups providing career guidance, psychometric profiling, or vocational training can partner as skill training assessment and placement partners.
- Eligible for government course fee subsidies and corporate CSR co-funding.
