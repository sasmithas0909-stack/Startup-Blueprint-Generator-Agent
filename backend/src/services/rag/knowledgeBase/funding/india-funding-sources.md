# Startup Funding Sources — India
Source: Various verified startup ecosystem resources
Last Verified: 2024

## Angel Networks & Early-Stage Investors

### Indian Angel Network (IAN)
**Type:** Angel Network
**Website:** https://www.indianangelnetwork.com
**Typical Investment:** INR 50 lakh – INR 10 crore
**Stage:** Pre-seed, Seed
**Focus:** Technology, consumer, healthcare, agri-tech
**Application:** Online application on website + pitch to network

### Mumbai Angels
**Type:** Angel Network
**Website:** https://www.mumbaiangels.com
**Typical Investment:** INR 25 lakh – INR 5 crore
**Stage:** Pre-seed, Seed
**Focus:** Technology-enabled businesses
**Application:** Submit pitch on website

### Venture Catalysts (VCats)
**Type:** Angel + Micro-VC
**Website:** https://www.venturecatalysts.in
**Typical Investment:** USD 100K – USD 1M (Seed)
**Stage:** Seed, Pre-Series A
**Focus:** Consumer tech, healthcare, fintech, edtech

### LetsVenture
**Type:** Angel Investment Platform
**Website:** https://www.letsventure.com
**Description:** Online platform connecting startups with angel investors
**Investment Range:** INR 25 lakh – INR 5 crore

## Government Funding Bodies

### Department of Science & Technology (DST) — NIDHI
**Type:** Government Grant + Equity
**Website:** https://nidhi.dst.gov.in
**Programs:**
- NIDHI-PRAYAS: Up to INR 10 lakh (Proof of Concept)
- NIDHI-EIR: Up to INR 30 lakh (Entrepreneur in Residence)
- NIDHI-Accelerator: Acceleration support + funding
**Stage:** Pre-seed, Seed
**Eligibility:** DPIIT recognised startups, based in India

### BIRAC (Biotechnology Industry Research Assistance Council)
**Type:** Government Grant
**Website:** https://www.birac.nic.in
**Focus:** Biotech, healthcare, life sciences
**Programs:**
- SPARSH: Up to INR 50 lakh
- BIG (Biotechnology Ignition Grant): Up to INR 50 lakh
**Stage:** Ideation, Early-stage

### STPI (Software Technology Parks of India)
**Type:** Government Support
**Website:** https://www.stpi.in
**Benefits:** Infrastructure support, tax incentives for IT/software startups
**Eligibility:** IT/software companies registered in India

## Incubators

### IIT Incubators (Multiple IITs)
**Type:** Incubator
**Programs:** SINE (IIT Bombay), IITM Pravartak (IIT Madras), TBIU (IIT Delhi)
**Support:** Seed funding INR 5-25 lakh, mentorship, infrastructure, networking
**Eligibility:** Tech startups, preference for IIT alumni but not mandatory
**Application:** Through respective IIT websites

### IIM Incubators
**Type:** Business Incubator
**Programs:** NSRCEL (IIM Bangalore), CIIE (IIM Ahmedabad)
**Support:** Mentorship, networking, seed funding, investor connects
**Focus:** Social enterprises, consumer tech, B2B

### T-Hub (Telangana)
**Type:** Government-backed Incubator/Accelerator
**Website:** https://t-hub.co
**Support:** Infrastructure, mentorship, corporate partnerships
**Eligibility:** All domains, Telangana preferred but not mandatory

### NASSCOM 10,000 Startups
**Type:** Industry-backed Program
**Website:** https://10000startups.com
**Support:** Mentorship, market access, cloud credits, investor network
**Focus:** Technology startups
**Eligibility:** Less than 5 years old, scalable tech product
